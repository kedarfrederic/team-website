Cookie Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

This page lists the cookies and similar storage Team uses, what each one is for, and how long it lasts. It covers teamrollouts.com and the Team app at app.teamrollouts.com.

Your choices

Only the cookies marked Necessary are set without asking. Analytics and marketing cookies are set only if you agree, and nothing in those categories is loaded or stored before you choose.

You can change your mind at any time — open your cookie preferences. Your choice is remembered in a single cookie shared across teamrollouts.com and app.teamrollouts.com, so you only have to make it once. You can also clear cookies in your browser at any point, which resets the choice and means you'll be asked again.

Refusing analytics and marketing cookies does not limit your access to the site or the product.

_fbp Meta (Facebook) Pixel Marketing Measures whether our advertising reaches the right people. Up to 3 months

Retention for third-party cookies is set by the provider and reflects their current documented defaults; the exact value can change on their side.

Third parties

Where a cookie is set by a provider rather than by us, that provider processes the data under its own policy: PostHog, Google, Meta and Clerk.

We don't sell personal information, and we don't use these cookies to build profiles for anyone other than ourselves.

Changes

If we add or remove a tool, this page changes with it and the date above is updated. If a change means we need to collect a new kind of consent, we'll ask again rather than assume your previous answer covers it.

Questions about this policy, or a request about your data, can go to our contact page. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms.
