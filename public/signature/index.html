Putting it in Gmail

  1. Gmail → Settings (the gear, top right) → See all settings.
  2. Stay on the General tab and scroll down to Signature.
  3. Click Create new, name it Team, then click into the big box and paste with ⌘V (or Ctrl+V).
  4. Underneath, set both dropdowns — For new emails use and On reply/forward use — to Team.
  5. Scroll to the very bottom and click Save Changes. This one is easy to miss, and nothing sticks without it.

The logo is built into the signature itself, so there's nothing to download and no image that can break later. Two blank lines are included above it on purpose — that's what stops the signature sitting under your cursor when you start a new message.

If the paste looks wrong — a black box, odd fonts — undo it, then paste again with ⌘⇧V. Still odd? Ask Kedar.
